The Philosophy of Edge AI

Most AI systems today live trapped inside distant datacenters. But the physical world doesn’t wait 300 milliseconds for a roundtrip API response when an industrial machine malfunctions, a crowd surges, or a vehicle navigates a blind corner.

My mission as an engineer is to miniaturize, quantize, and embed neural intelligence directly where the sensors live — onto NVIDIA Jetson microcomputers, microcontrollers, and localized edge servers.
